Your agreement and your patients

Each clinic signs a short data agreement with Cyvo before we go live. Your clinic tells its patients that appointments are handled on WhatsApp with Cyvo’s help.

The Clinic Vitals checkup

The 1-minute checkup at Clinic Vitals asks 8 questions about how your clinic works. It never asks about patients. Your answers stay in your browser until you ask for your report.

When you ask for your report, we store: your name, your clinic’s name, your WhatsApp number or email, your 8 answers, your score and estimate, which version of the consent wording you agreed to, and where you came from (for example Instagram or our website). We keep them in a Google Sheet that only Cyvo can open.

We use them to: send your report, and message you about it and a founding place at most 3 times. Your report link holds only a random code, not your name.

Counting visits: the page records how many people reach each step (for example “question 3” or “score shown”), with only the source of the visit and no answers or contact details. It uses no cookies, ad trackers or tracking pixels.

Combined results: we may publish combined, anonymised results, for example “7 in 10 Mumbai dental clinics don’t reply after 9 pm”. No clinic or person is ever named.

Stopping and deleting: reply STOP on WhatsApp, or use the unsubscribe link in our email, and we stop. If you don’t reply to our messages, we delete your details 90 days after we mark them inactive. If you opt out, after 90 days we keep only your number or email and the fact that you opted out, so we never message you again. To have everything deleted, message us and we do it within 7 days and confirm.
